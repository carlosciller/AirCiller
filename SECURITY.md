# Security and privacy

## Reporting a vulnerability

This repository does not yet have a private security channel. Before making it public, enable **Private vulnerability reporting** in GitHub. Until then, do not open a public issue that reveals credentials, private network addresses, or personal data.

## Data that must not be attached

- Pairing codes or AirPlay credentials.
- Keychain exports.
- Names or addresses of devices on a home network.
- Full paths containing usernames or titles of private files.
- Movies, audio, or subtitles protected by third-party rights.

Review and anonymize logs before sharing them. If a credential may have been exposed, delete it from the Keychain and authorize the Apple TV again.
