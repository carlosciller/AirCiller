# AirCiller 0.18.0

Fixes for HDR startup, Playlist controls and watching consecutive episodes.

- Fix direct HDR preparation failing or using excessive memory when audio
  precedes video in the MP4. Video, audio and their sample positions are preserved.
- Keep the Mac awake between episodes and while waiting for audio approval.
  A conversion choice can cover equivalent following episodes in the same
  session. Stop ends that approval.
- Adding files to Playlist now preserves the movie waiting for authorization
  or an audio decision. New items join the queue without replacing it.
- Move a selected season with the Playlist menu or keyboard commands, then undo
  the move. Collapsing a season with a selected episode selects its parent.
- Honor the SDH preference when matching text and bitmap subtitles. An available
  SDH track takes priority; bitmap subtitles still need local OCR on first use.
- When track settings change elsewhere while you are editing, the panel explains
  the conflict and offers **Load Current Tracks**. Converted audio no longer
  displays the original track's Atmos label or channel count.
- Prevent a cancelled direct-MP4 startup from resuming after a delayed operation.
  Cancelling AirPlay authorization also releases its wake assertion.
- Add opt-in private control through a separately configured ChatGPT connector.
  It can control playback and open or close AirCiller while the Mac is awake and
  logged in, including with the screen locked. It cannot wake or unlock the Mac.
  Setup requires your own relay and credentials; no hosted service is included.
- Block subtitle-service redirects that could disclose credentials, and reject
  incorrect local-installation destinations before replacing an app.
- Update certifi to 2026.10.19 and multidict to 6.9.1 for upstream security fixes.
  FFmpeg, Python and pyatv remain unchanged.

Compatible original audio stays preferred. Audio conversion still requires your
choice; video is not re-encoded. No new media formats are introduced.
