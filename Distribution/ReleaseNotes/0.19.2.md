# AirCiller 0.19.2

Opening the same movie again can now reuse its prepared direct MP4, saving the
work of preparing it again when the file and selected tracks have not changed.

- Direct MP4 and HLS preparations share the existing cache limit in Settings >
  Storage. Turning the cache off or clearing it applies to both.
- Files with large embedded attachments take less work to inspect. Bitmap
  subtitle preparation uses faster lossless image compression before recognition.
- Fixed a missing final bitmap subtitle when its track has no closing clear event.
- Sending the same track selection or synchronization values through private
  control no longer interrupts playback to prepare the movie again.

Formats and bundled engines are unchanged. The cache benefit applies to local
preparation, not the time Apple TV takes to start playback. Intermittent receiver
discovery remains under investigation.

[Scope and validation](https://github.com/carlosciller/AirCiller/blob/v0.19.2/Docs/RELEASE_0.19.2.md).
