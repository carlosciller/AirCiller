# AirCiller 0.18.2

Less waiting when a large HDR movie is still being analyzed as you press Play.

- Direct MP4 preparation now waits for unfinished bitrate analysis to stop,
  so the two tasks no longer compete for the same file. Completed analysis is
  kept. The benefit depends on the file and whether analysis is still running.
- Cancelling preparation closes the analysis process and its readers. An
  immediate retry or change of movie waits for that cleanup to finish.
- Playback information now distinguishes estimated bitrate peaks from measured
  ones, and says when a peak is unavailable.

Video and audio quality, supported formats and playback engines are unchanged.
Measurements found less local preparation time on a large HDR sample; they do
not establish the same saving for every movie or for time to first picture.
