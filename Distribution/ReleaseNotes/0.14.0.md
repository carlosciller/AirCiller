# AirCiller 0.14.0

A redesigned main window brings your library, current movie and playback controls together.

- Playlist and Recents stay in the sidebar. The current movie has a larger title, a timeline with elapsed and total time, and a central play/pause button. Select another movie to see its details without interrupting playback.
- Open **Audio & Subtitles** to choose tracks in a side panel named for the current movie. Expand **Synchronization** for timing adjustments or **Audio Options** for output choices. **Apply Changes** becomes available when you make an edit; **Cancel** discards it.
- The window can be made smaller. Long filenames wrap in the session and selection details, and chapter controls adapt to the available space.
- Use **Command-Left/Right** to skip ten seconds, or **Option-Command-Left/Right** for thirty seconds. Unmodified arrow keys remain available for lists and controls. Playlist reordering shortcuts are unchanged.
- **Stop** remains available during analysis, preparation and the brief wait before automatic playback. Duplicate playback commands are disabled while those operations or authorization are pending.
- Clearing Playlist or Recents now asks for confirmation and explains what will be removed. Your original movie files stay in place.
- OpenSubtitles distinguishes a failed search from a search with no matches. If a bundled playback tool is missing, the error explains that AirCiller needs to be reinstalled.

The app icon, playback engines and supported formats are unchanged.

Known limitation: manual audio synchronization in SDR HLS can still leave the requested offset unapplied. Subtitle synchronization is separate.
