# AirCiller 0.19.0

Select a season to see the next unwatched episode and its saved position, then
choose **Continue Season**. AirCiller follows your manual Playlist order. A
completed season stays completed until you choose an episode to watch again.

- **Skip Intro** and **Skip Recap** appear inside explicitly named, valid file
  chapters. They jump to the marked end; generic chapter numbers do not qualify.
- Recognized single-episode filenames have shorter names in season folders.
  Right-click an episode and choose **Edit Display Name** to correct or reset
  its name. Undo is available. The original filename stays in selection details;
  the file itself is not renamed.
- **Playback Information** separates the original format, chosen preparation,
  completed local conversion and receiver state. Long details wrap and scroll.
  File metadata and local checks do not certify the Apple TV's HDMI format or
  physical Atmos/HDR output.

Formats, bundled engines and audio/video preparation are unchanged.
[Scope and validation](https://github.com/carlosciller/AirCiller/blob/v0.19.0/Docs/RELEASE_0.19.0.md).
