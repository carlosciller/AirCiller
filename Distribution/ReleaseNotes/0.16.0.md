# AirCiller 0.16.0

PCM soundtracks can now be sent as lossless FLAC. Audio and subtitle controls also stay within reach in the sidebar.

- In **Audio & Subtitles > Audio Options**, choose **Convert PCM to lossless FLAC**. It preserves the soundtrack's samples, sample rate and channels while leaving the video and original file unchanged. Supported inputs are integer PCM and Blu-ray LPCM at 16 or 24 bits, with known mono, stereo, side-channel 5.1 or 7.1 layouts.
- **Preserve original** remains the default. FLAC conversion is an explicit choice, available through HLS with manual audio synchronization set to zero. It is unavailable for HDR/Dolby Vision with subtitles on the direct MP4 route, rear-channel 5.1, unknown layouts and 20-bit LPCM.
- **Audio & Subtitles** opens by default. Hide or show it with the toolbar button. Applying changes keeps it open; opening another movie refreshes the controls and discards unapplied selections from the previous file.

The bundled engines are unchanged. Developer ID and notarization are still unavailable, so a first download may require approval in macOS before opening. Existing SDR manual audio-timing limitations remain.
