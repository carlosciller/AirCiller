# AirCiller 0.10.5

This release makes setup and Apple TV authorization more dependable.

### Improvements

- Checks the complete AirPlay engine before reporting it as ready.
- Repairs an installed component without losing the previous working version.
- Cancels short AirPlay operations cleanly when they are no longer needed.

### Fixes

- Prevents overlapping Apple TV credential changes during authorization.
- Recovers safely if Playlist or Recents data becomes damaged.
- Rejects component downloads that do not match their expected size.
