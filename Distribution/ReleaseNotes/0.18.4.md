# AirCiller 0.18.4

Choose a fallback subtitle language in Settings > Playback. For example, prefer
Spanish and use English when no Spanish track matches your subtitle preference.
The choice applies when you open a movie; it does not change the current track.
Automatic subtitles stay off when disabled.

AirCiller now waits for the previous AirPlay helper to close before starting a
replacement after Stop or a playback change. A cancelled start cannot continue
afterward. This prevents overlapping helper processes; it does not establish
the cause of every receiver startup timeout.

Formats, bundled engines and audio/video preparation are unchanged.
[Scope and validation](https://github.com/carlosciller/AirCiller/blob/v0.18.4/Docs/RELEASE_0.18.4.md).
