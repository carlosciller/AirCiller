# AirCiller 0.15.0

Add a season folder to Playlist and keep its episodes together, with saved order,
watched status and playback progress.

- Drop a folder into Playlist or choose it with **Add Movie…**. Expand the season
  to see its episodes. Filenames are sorted naturally, so Episode 2 comes before
  Episode 10. Subfolders and extras appear in the same season; hidden files and
  symbolic links are skipped.
- Drag episodes to reorder them within a season, or move a whole season together.
  The insertion line shows where the item will go. Keyboard reordering and
  **Edit > Undo / Redo** remain available.
- Right-click an episode or season to mark it watched or unwatched. Finishing an
  episode marks it watched before moving to the next Playlist item. Removing a
  season removes its library entries, not its original files, and can be undone.
- Movies opened individually now enter Playlist before analysis. If analysis or
  playback fails, the entry stays available. Adding movies or folders leaves an
  existing analysis, preparation or playback session running.
- Folder imports show progress and can be cancelled. Reimporting a folder adds
  new files without duplicates; when nothing is new, the receipt says so.
  Unreadable or empty folders are reported instead of silently ignored.

The bundled engines and supported media formats are unchanged. The existing SDR
HLS manual-audio-timing limitation remains; subtitle timing is separate.
